About FLWRPWR

FLWRPWR is a cannabis brand built on good vibes, great flower, and a deeper purpose: to connect people through high-quality, responsibly sourced flower and a community rooted in wellness.

  • Premium flower
  • Lab tested
  • No harsh chemicals
  • No trim, no shake

Our Mission

“Be present, feel better, grow together.”

Founded in Southern California in 2026, FLWRPWR is a women-owned cannabis brand rooted in quality flower, wellness, and community.

We believe quality begins with intention. Our flower is selected from trusted farms, using only whole cannabis flower, never trim or shake. The care we put into our products extends beyond cannabis to the people who support us and the earth we call home.

Our commitment to giving back is an extension of who we are. By supporting environmental restoration and advocating for survivors of domestic violence and sexual assault, we hope to create a positive impact that extends beyond our flower.

As FLWRPWR continues to grow, we want to create a welcoming community where everyone feels supported and part of a larger purpose.

Good vibes, great flower. Together, we grow.
